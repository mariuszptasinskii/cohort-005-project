# Team licences are refunded all or nothing, by revoking their coupons

A team purchase can only be refunded while none of its seats are claimed, and the Refund is always the full amount paid. Approval marks the purchase's coupons as revoked instead of deleting them, so the team page can show them as refunded and a code someone shared explains why it no longer works.

## Considered Options

- **Partial refund for the unclaimed seats.** Rejected: contradicts "a Refund is always the full amount paid" and needs per-seat money handling. It can be its own feature later.
